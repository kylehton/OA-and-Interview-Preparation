## **Technical Round 1 Questions for Interviewers/Engineers**

- If I were to have been hired for this role, what traits and contributions would I have had to embody during my time interning at Khan Academy for you to look back and think, “I made a good decision in selecting him”?

- Job hopping is a pretty common thing nowadays, especially in the tech sector and in software engineering. However, I definitely think Khan Academy is different from a large majority of tech companies. So, what is the one thing that tends to make people stay here longer than they might have expected to?

- Is there any information you can share about the team I would be placed on? Out of curiosity, I would love to know what type of product or feature I would contribute to.

- What traits do people that thrive on your teams have?

- What is a current team challenge and how are you currently working through it with your team?

- How would success be measured in the role after 90 days?

- What is something about working here that you didn’t expect when you had first joined?

- How do engineers here stay connected to the learners and educators who use the platform? Are there feedback loops between the technical and education teams?

- At a company that places learning and improvement at the forefront, what does good code review culture look like here? How do engineers at Khan Academy give each other feedback?

- What’s something that excites you most about the direction Khan Academy is going right now? Is it more in-line with feature advancements, like Khanmigo, or with increase in exposure, like through planned collaborations with platforms like Canvas and Google Classroom?

- In your opinion, with how easily knowledge is, and how information is widespread across the internet, especially accessible through YouTube videos and LLMs, what differentiates Khan Academy from other tech platforms that can be used for education, besides being free and accessible?



## **Technical Round 2 Questions for Interviewers/Engineers**

- I’ve read that Khan Academy handles a lot of data from learners around the world. How does the team approach scalability and reliability when serving millions of students, especially in regions with limited internet access?

- In my previous interview round, Ms. Third touched up on how Khan Academy stores a lot of user learning data for usage. How does Khan Academy balance between being a learning platform and a data platform — for example, using learner data to improve education while keeping privacy and ethics central?

- What kinds of engineering challenges are most unique to Khan Academy compared to other ed-tech or consumer web products?

- What’s an example of a technical or design decision that looked small at first but ended up having a surprisingly big impact on learners or the product?

- From my understanding last interview, engineers work pretty close with each other in the software development and revision cycle, allowing for a mixture of implementations and ideas in creating new functionalities. How closely do engineers collaborate with product designers and educators when developing new features? I would imagine that this would be critical for both user experience and genuine impact for the effectiveness of Khan Academy as a platform.

- For someone who does well in the internship, what kinds of opportunities or next steps are typically available afterward?

- Is there any upcoming technical initiative or product area that the team is especially excited about right now?

- How does Khan Academy evaluate the success of its engineering initiatives (ie. is it more about learner engagement, technical metrics, or a mixture)?

- If I were to join the team and wanted to make a real contribution in my first few weeks — not just code, but something that meaningfully improves how the team works or learns — what kind of initiative would you love to see from an intern?
