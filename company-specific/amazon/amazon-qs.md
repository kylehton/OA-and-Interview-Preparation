# Questions for Regulations + Data Compliance Org. Interviewers - Amazon

1. How does the team evaluate the quality of agent outputs in a compliance-type setting, where being correct and data-compliant really matters? Are there some sort of metrics involved, or is it a human-based output review system?

2. 2ND INTERVIEWER ONLY: From your perspective running the organization, how do you balance shipping new features while ensuring and building on current auditability and compliance requirements of the platform? I would imagine there is a desire to move fast and build, but also make everything more defensible.

3. The application stack is essentially all serverless. Why did the team choose to build the platform on a serverless model, and has there ever been any instances where the team has run into usage limits or bottlenecks?

4. What is something that you really like about this team that most people wouldn’t think of or be able to deduce with outside information?

5. Let’s say I was given the role as the intern for this team. What contributions would I had to make in order for you to think back to this moment and say, “I made a good decision in hiring this person for the position”?
